Fragrance Journal

Good Fragrance Is More Than a Luxury — It's a Personality.

The scent you wear is more than just a way to smell good; it's an expression of who you are. The right scent express your style, show your confidence and leave a memorable impression. Explore fresh, floral, woody and oriental to find a fragrance that feels like you.

Choose a scent by mood

Choosing a fragrance is easier when you begin with the mood you want to create. Fresh and citrus feels bright, florals bring a softer character, while woods and warm spices add depth. Try a scent on skin and give it time to develop before deciding.

Make fragrance part of your routine

Apply fragrance lightly to clean skin and store the bottle somewhere cool and shaded. Explore formats—from perfume oils to body mists—to find an application style that feels right for you, and follow the directions on each product. NOTE SMELLING GOOD IS A GOOD BUSINESS
